Firmware for ESP32 e-readers

Manga on e‑ink. No computer.

PaperManga finds manga online by itself, downloads chapters and fits the pages to your reader's screen. On models with PSRAM, chapters are prepared right on the reader. For the older X4 and X3, a computer prepares them.

Free · new ports 0.5.1 · the tested X4 Classic version is available separately

Three steps to your first chapter

On models with PSRAM, the catalog and chapter preparation run on the reader itself.

01

Install

Connect your reader to a computer and press the button in the Install section. It takes a couple of minutes.

02

Connect Wi‑Fi

Open Wi‑Fi on the reader. It creates its own network: join it from your phone and enter your home Wi‑Fi password. On the X4 Classic, networks saved in CrossPoint are picked up automatically.

03

Find and read

Open Catalog, find a manga and pick a chapter. On the older X4 and X3, prepare a black-and-white XTC in the computer app and send it over Wi‑Fi or on the memory card.

Catalog

Find manga right on the reader

Popular, new and search by title. Pick a chapter and the reader downloads it and fits the pages to the screen.

  • MangaDex: no account needed.
  • MangaLib: with your own account, including 18+.
  • Download several chapters at once and read offline.
  • Long manhwa strips are split into screens, and spreads into halves.
Library

All your series on one shelf

Every series has its own cover. New and read chapters are marked, and a chapter you started opens where you left off.

  • At the bottom you can see how much space is left on the card.
  • You can delete just the chapters you have read, or the whole series.
Reading

Nothing on screen but manga

On our X4 Classic with UC8279, fast page turns without flashing have been tested. On other boards, speed and refresh modes still need testing. The clock and page number are optional.

  • A one-button menu: jump around, chapter list, clear the screen.
  • At the end of a chapter, the next one opens with a single press.

Install

You need a computer with Chrome or Edge and a USB cable that carries data, not just power.

Checking available builds

  1. Connect the reader to your computer and turn it on.
  2. Press the button below and choose the reader's USB port. Its name depends on the model.
  3. In the “Erase device” window, do not tick the box. Press “Next”, then “Install”. In about two minutes the reader restarts with PaperManga.

Before you install
  • The browser checks the processor family, but not the board model. So double-check the model you choose yourself.
  • PaperManga is tested on our X4 Classic with UC8279. The other versions are experimental: they build, but have not been tested on real devices.
  • Back up the factory firmware before an experimental install. Do not tick “Erase device”: it wipes your settings and the screen's factory calibration. Your memory card is not formatted.
  • Don't close the page or unplug the cable while the install is running.
  • If the browser can't see the reader, the seller may have locked the firmware. Don't try to get around this with third-party tools.
  • To restore your reader, you need firmware made for your exact model. You can install CrossPoint from its website if your device is supported.

FAQ

What happens to CrossPoint?

In the tested X4 Classic version, it stays next to PaperManga. The experimental ports use a standalone PaperManga with its own updates.

How do I sign in to MangaLib?

Connect the reader to Wi‑Fi and open Wi‑Fi on the home screen. It shows an address: open it in your phone's browser and sign in to your account.

How do I update the firmware?

Open Info and press Check. The update is picked for your exact board. Inkplate 6 with 4 MB flash is updated over USB through this site.

What can the X3 and older X4 do?

The lite version reads black-and-white XTC. Prepare chapters in the computer app and send them over Wi‑Fi or on the memory card.

How does touch control work?

In menus, swipes move the selection and a tap picks an item. The bottom bar has Back, ◀, ▶ and Open. While reading, tapping the edges turns pages and tapping the middle opens the menu. A long touch goes back; holding Open on the bottom bar triggers the hold action.

Where does the manga come from?

From services you connect yourself: MangaDex and MangaLib. PaperManga doesn't store or share anything. The rights to the manga belong to its authors and publishers.
